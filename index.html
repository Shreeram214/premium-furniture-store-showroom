About WOODORA LIVING

Beautiful Spaces Begin With the Right Furniture.

At WOODORA LIVING, we believe furniture is more than something you place in a room. It defines how you live, relax, work and connect with the people around you.

Our Sangli showroom brings together sofas, beds, wardrobes, dining sets, office furniture and decor pieces chosen for comfort, everyday practicality and a calm, modern look. Walk through real room settings, feel the fabrics and finishes, and talk to our team about what suits your space.

  • Curated living, bedroom, dining and office ranges
  • Custom sizes, materials and colours on request
  • Friendly, no-pressure guidance in the showroom
